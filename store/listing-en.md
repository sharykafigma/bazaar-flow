## Full description (max 4000)
Keep the goods moving and never let the belt jam! 📦

Belt Jam is a colorful conveyor sort puzzle where every tap matters. Tap a crate in the warehouse, send its goods rolling onto the loop belt, and watch each market shop grab exactly the colors it ordered. Plan your moves, keep the belt flowing, and close every shop before the belt jams!

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the front crate in any warehouse column to load its goods onto the belt
• Shops take only the color they are asking for, and only as many as they need
• Finish every shop order to clear the level
• Fill the belt with goods nobody wants and it's a Belt Jam, so think one step ahead

FEATURES
• Hundreds of levels that get trickier as you go
• Easy to learn, hard to master: a calm sort puzzle with real strategy
• Mystery crates, locked shops and new twists as you progress
• Short levels, perfect for a quick break
• Play offline, no Wi-Fi needed
• Bright, warm market art and satisfying conveyor sounds
• Free to play

If you enjoy color sort, conveyor, traffic jam, bus jam or match puzzles, Belt Jam brings a fresh conveyor twist to your daily brain training.

Open your stall, keep the goods flowing and become the master of the market!
