## Full description (max 4000)
Welcome to the busiest bazaar in town! 🛒

Bazaar Flow is a colorful conveyor puzzle where every tap matters. Tap a crate in the godown, send its goods rolling onto the loop belt, and watch each shop grab exactly the colors it ordered. Plan your moves, keep the belt moving, and close every shop before the belt jams!

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the front crate in any godown column to load its goods onto the belt
• Shops take only the color they are asking for, and only as many as they need
• Finish every shop order to clear the level
• Fill the belt with goods nobody wants and it's a Belt Jam, so think one step ahead

FEATURES
• Hundreds of levels that get trickier as you go
• Easy to learn, hard to master: a calm sort puzzle with real strategy
• Mystery crates, locked shops and new bazaar twists as you progress
• Short levels, perfect for a quick break
• Play offline, no Wi-Fi needed
• Bright, warm bazaar art and satisfying conveyor sounds
• Free to play

If you enjoy color sort, conveyor, traffic jam or match puzzles, Bazaar Flow brings a fresh market twist to your daily brain training.

Open your stall, keep the goods flowing and become the king of the bazaar!
