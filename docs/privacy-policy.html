Belt Jam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Belt Jam ("the game", "we") handles information. The game is published on Google Play by Shary Studio.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game has no account or login. Your level progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads (including optional rewarded videos) through third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob and/or Unity LevelPlay and their mediation partners. These partners may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads seen or tapped) and crash or performance diagnostics

They use this to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) we ask for your consent before personalised ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads.

Partner policies: Google, Unity.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock the item.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We may use anonymous analytics and crash reporting to see which levels are too hard and to fix bugs. This data is not used to identify you.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data security and retention

Data sent to our partners is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Partners keep data according to their own policies.

7. Your choices and deleting data

You can uninstall the game at any time to remove all locally stored data. To ask us about any data related to you, email us at the address below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
